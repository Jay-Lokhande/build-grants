# Grant & fellowship scams (red flags)

Not legal advice. When in doubt, **do not pay** and verify the org independently.

## Red flags

- “Guaranteed grant” for an upfront fee or “processing” payment  
- PDF / WhatsApp “invitation” not listed on the foundation’s official site  
- `@gmail.com` “program officer” for a well-known foundation  
- Crypto wallet address to “unlock” funding  
- Copy-paste of NLnet / Mozilla / EU logos with a random `.com` apply link  

## Safer habits

1. Type the URL yourself or use links from [README.md](../README.md) only.  
2. Cross-check [NLnet](https://nlnet.nl/), [Mozilla Foundation](https://foundation.mozilla.org/), [GitHub Security](https://github.com/security) news posts.  
3. Report phishing: foundation abuse contacts + your platform (GitHub, email provider).
